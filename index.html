Real breed, real facts

The Lykoi, the werewolf cat

01

Named for the wolf

“Lykoi” comes from the Greek word for wolf. Look one straight in the face and you'll see why.

02

A natural mutation

The look isn't designer-made. It's a naturally occurring recessive gene, first documented in kittens from ordinary black domestic shorthairs in 2010.

03

Started in 2011

The breeding program began in 2011 in Tennessee, led by veterinarian Dr. Johnny Gobble with Brittney Gobble and Patti Thomas.

04

The only roan cat

The coat mixes solid black hairs with white ones. That pattern is called roan, and TICA lists the Lykoi as the only recognized cat breed with it.

05

The werewolf mask

Lykoi have little or no hair around the eyes, nose, muzzle, and chin, and almost no undercoat. Most will molt much of their coat at least once.

06

Show-ring royalty

The International Cat Association (TICA) granted the Lykoi full championship status in May 2017. Only solid black roan is shown.

07

Sun-kissed skin

Their skin is pink, but it can darken with sunlight or heat, then return to pink when the sun is scarce.

08

Friendly by standard

The breed standard describes the Lykoi as a very friendly cat that demands attention from people.

Sources: TICA breed profile, GCCF, Buckley et al., Genes (2020).
