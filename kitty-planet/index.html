Lycan the Lykoi Sets a Place for the Pack

A candlelit Thanksgiving dispatch from the court of Catropolis

Catropolis — The invitation was formal. The guest of honor arrived in court dress: a simple ball-point crown, a gold collar with a diamond paw tag, and a pair of yellow eyes already conducting a thorough inquiry of the pumpkin pie.

Lycan the Lykoi took his seat at a candlelit Thanksgiving table this week, autumn leaves at his paws and a cornucopia he had, I am reliably informed, already sniffed twice and ruled “adequate.” Gold circuit lines glowed behind the candles, a palace lit by tradition and a little static. He did not leap on the pie. He did consider it. In this reporter’s notebook, that counts as royal restraint.

The scene was warm, a little formal, and unmistakably Lycan: regal posture, feral whiskers, and a loyalty you can feel even when he is pretending not to stare at dessert.

A Letter from the King

A thank-you to the pack

Pack,

I have sat at a fine table and remembered the only thing that makes a court worth keeping: you.

I was born a little rough around the ears, and I remain so, crown or no crown. A king may be dressed in gold and still think, for one traitorous second, about putting a paw in the pie. I did not. Barely. You may add that to my legend if you must.

This season I offer no fanfare. I offer thanks. Thank you for showing up, for staying decent, and for treating this pack like a pack. I will keep the crown polished and the pie mostly untouched. The chair beside me is yours.

With a low growl and a full heart,

— Lycan
